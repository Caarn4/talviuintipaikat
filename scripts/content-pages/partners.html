Make your venue stand out

A distinctive partner marker helps people spot your venue as they explore the map. While a standard listing shows the name, location and a link for more information, a partner listing adds photos, a short introduction and details about your facilities.

Together, we’ll agree where your venue will be featured: on the map and on relevant city and regional pages.

The listing examples show a fictional venue. Your listing will use your own photos and information approved together. Partner placements are clearly labelled.
